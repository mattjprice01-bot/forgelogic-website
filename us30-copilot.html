THE COPILOT MODEL

Four states. One clear distinction.

Copilot is designed not to turn every directional observation into an entry. It progresses through explicit states as market evidence develops.

01

SCANNING

No qualified direction. The engine continues evaluating the market.

02

BIAS IDENTIFIED

A directional structure has qualified, but this is explicitly not an entry.

03

ENTRY READY

Entry conditions have qualified. Planned levels can then be presented.

04

LIVE TRADE

After the user confirms an actual entry, Copilot changes role to position monitoring.

BIAS ≠ ENTRY

Designed to reduce chasing.

When direction is identified but entry confirmation is absent, Copilot says so plainly. Entry zone, invalidation and targets remain withheld until an entry-ready plan exists.

LIVE MARKET MAP

Price action with Copilot context.

The market map keeps the live chart and analytical state in one workflow. Copilot levels appear only when an entry-ready plan exists, rather than continuously projecting trade levels.

OPPORTUNITY ENGINE

Qualified setups, not noise.

The setup view separates structure, macro context, futures confirmation and entry readiness, while giving the user a concise explanation of why the engine is in its current state.

LIVE TRADE COPILOT

From entry to exit.

Once the user has actually entered and manually confirms “I'm in”, Copilot stops hunting for a new opportunity and begins monitoring the active position while validation continues in the background.
