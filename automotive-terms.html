Your subscription

ForgeLogic Automotive AI Assistant provides AI guidance for vehicle faults, maintenance and practical tasks, with vehicle records, job history, photo questions and supported voice features. It is supplied by ForgeLogic Technologies Ltd. Contact contact@forgelogic-tech.co.uk for billing, complaints or support.

The advertised subscription is £9.99 per month. Review the total payable on Stripe checkout before confirming. Payment activates access for the account used to purchase. This is separate from US30 Copilot and other ForgeLogic products. Installing the app is free and does not activate paid features.

Agreement and immediate access

At Stripe checkout you must actively accept these Automotive terms and request immediate access before payment. Your acceptance and this contract version are recorded against the Stripe checkout session. Requesting immediate access does not waive your statutory cancellation rights. Your payment receipt and billing history are available in My Account, Manage billing.

Renewal and cancellation

Your subscription renews monthly until cancelled. To stop future renewals, open the assistant, choose My Account, then Manage billing and cancel the subscription. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the paid billing period. If you cannot use the portal, email us with the email address used for your account. Do not send card details.

Consumer cancellation rights

Stopping renewal and exercising a statutory cancellation right are separate. Nothing on this page removes your statutory consumer rights. UK distance-contract cancellation rights may apply, including a 14-day cancellation period starting when the contract is entered into. To exercise a cancellation right or request a refund, email us with a clear statement that you wish to cancel, your account email and purchase date. You do not have to use a specific form. We will assess the request under the applicable law, including rights relating to a service that is faulty or not as described.

Compatibility and usage

Use a current supported browser and an internet connection. Installation and microphone features depend on your browser and device. AI allowances are currently 20 units per day and 200 per month per account; research, photos and voice can consume additional units. Shared daily capacity limits apply. Provider outages and rate limits may temporarily interrupt AI features.

Using guidance safely

AI answers and photo interpretations may be incomplete or incorrect. Confirm the exact vehicle, variant, procedure, tools and manufacturer specifications before work. Guidance does not certify a repair or establish roadworthiness. Stop and obtain qualified help if the task exceeds your knowledge or equipment, or involves a safety risk. No diagnostic success or repair outcome is guaranteed.
