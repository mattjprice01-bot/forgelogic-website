Automotive privacy notice

Updated: 4 November 2026

This notice explains the Automotive AI Assistant and supplements our general privacy policy. ForgeLogic Technologies Ltd is responsible for the service. Contact contact@forgelogic-tech.co.uk about privacy or your information.

Information used

We process your account identity and contact details, vehicle registration and specifications, saved jobs, questions, checks, AI responses, uploaded photos, subscription status and technical security records. Account details are needed for sign-in; an active subscription is needed for paid features. Photos and dictation are optional.

How the assistant works

Relevant questions, vehicle details, recent conversation context and selected photos are sent to Google Gemini to generate guidance. Vehicle and task details, and relevant observations, may also be sent for online research. Text selected for natural spoken replies is sent to Google for speech generation. Browser dictation may use your browser or device provider's speech-recognition service; its handling depends on that provider. You can type instead.

Vehicle registration lookups use the configured DVSA vehicle-data service. Stripe processes payments and billing management; ForgeLogic stores subscription and payment identifiers and status rather than collecting card numbers in its registration form. Railway hosts the application, database and private photo storage. Following an external video or source link takes you to that provider.

Purpose and lawful basis

We use account, vehicle, job and subscription information to provide the service and manage your contract. We process proportionate security and abuse-prevention information for our legitimate interest in protecting accounts and service availability. We retain records needed to meet legal and accounting obligations. Optional browser permissions can be declined; refusing microphone access does not prevent typed questions.

Photos and saved records

Photos are resized and re-encoded for storage and can be sent to the AI provider when selected for a question. Access to stored photos is restricted to the owning account. Avoid uploading faces, addresses, documents, payment details or unrelated personal information. Saved vehicle and job records support your ongoing work. Case deletion removes associated database records and attempts to remove its stored photos and research; failed cleanup can require follow-up. Backup copies and provider-held records may have separate retention periods.

Your choices and rights

Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability where applicable. You can separately object to processing based on legitimate interests; explain your circumstances in your request. Cancelling billing does not itself request deletion of your account or saved work. You can raise a concern with us or complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies and device storage

The assistant uses sign-in cookies and browser storage for preferences, onboarding and installation/offline assets. Offline assets do not make AI guidance available without an internet connection.

For general rights and privacy information, see the general privacy policy. Contact us for details of retention periods, processing locations and transfer safeguards. Private application storage does not prevent selected information being processed by the AI provider.
