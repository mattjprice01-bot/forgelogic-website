Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Trading speculative or leveraged products can result in substantial losses.

1. Nature of US30 Copilot

US30 Copilot is market-analysis and decision-support software. It can present analytical states, scores, readiness assessments, market context and potential trade-related levels. These outputs are not guarantees of future market performance.

2. User control

US30 Copilot does not connect to brokerage accounts, transmit or execute orders, hold client money or determine a user's position size. Users independently decide whether, when and how to trade.

3. Analytical risk

Outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed, stale or invalidated by changing market conditions. LONG/SHORT states, scores, readiness assessments, entry areas, invalidation levels or targets should not be interpreted as certainty.

4. Market risk

Market prices can move rapidly and unpredictably. Stop-loss orders or intended exit levels may not execute at the expected price.

5. Leverage risk

Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Users are responsible for understanding leverage, margin and product risks.

6. Data and technology risk

US30 Copilot depends on software, networks and third-party data. Data may be unavailable, delayed, interrupted or incorrect.

7. Behavioural risk

Users may over-trade, use excessive leverage, misunderstand outputs or place undue reliance on the software.

8. No performance guarantee

ForgeLogic does not guarantee profits, win rates, accuracy, returns or avoidance of loss.

9. Suitability

US30 Copilot may not be suitable for every person. Users should only trade products they understand.
